## Research

Kamitani Lab studies how the brain represents the world, developing techniques to decode and visualize mental contents from brain activity. We combine **functional MRI (fMRI)**, **machine learning**, and **deep neural networks** to investigate neural information processing underlying perception, imagery, dreams, and cognition.

Our goals include:
- Understanding basic principles of neural information representation
- Developing brain decoding and brain-machine interface technologies
- Bridging neuroscience and artificial intelligence (NeuroAI)

## Affiliation

The lab is part of the **Department of Intelligence Science and Technology**, Graduate School of Informatics, Kyoto University. We also maintain close collaboration with the **ATR Computational Neuroscience Laboratories**, where Prof. Kamitani serves as Department Head.

The lab was established at Kyoto University in April 2015.
