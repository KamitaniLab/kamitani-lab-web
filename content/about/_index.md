## Affiliation

The lab is part of the **Department of Intelligence Science and Technology**, Graduate School of Informatics, **Kyoto University**.

We also maintain close collaboration with the **ATR Computational Neuroscience Laboratories**, where Prof. Kamitani serves as Department Head.

## Admission

Graduate students are admitted through the Department of Intelligence Science and Technology, Graduate School of Informatics, Kyoto University.

- [Admissions Information (Graduate School of Informatics)](https://www.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/admission/)
- [Department of Intelligence Science and Technology](https://www.ist.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/)
