Brain signals can be seen as “codes” that encode our mental contents. We study methods for modeling brain functions and representations using information science and technology including machine learning and artificial neural networks. Our approach is based on data-driven predictive models that relate brain data and mind states via analysis of massive neural, behavioral, and multimedia data. Using these models, we aim to understand basic principles of neural information processing, and seek to develop real-life applications such as brain–machine interfaces that exploit decoded brain information.
