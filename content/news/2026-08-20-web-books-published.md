Three open web books written in the lab are now freely available online (in Japanese).

**[読む・書く・伝える：研究の実践ガイド](https://kamitanilab.github.io/research-handbook/)** — A practical guide for students joining the lab: research ethics, reading papers and managing references, writing, presentation, English, and publishing research.

**[脳とAIをつなぐ数理 ― 表現・学習・生成](https://kamitanilab.github.io/neuroAI-math-book/)** — An introduction to the mathematics shared by brains and artificial neural networks: neuronal dynamics, encoding and decoding, learning and generalization, representational geometry, generative models, and what "representation" means.

**[統計入門 数理サプリメント](https://kamitanilab.github.io/stats-math-supplement/)** — A mathematical companion to the introductory statistics course: derivations behind the formulas, probability distributions, Bayesian statistics, and first steps in causal inference and machine learning.
