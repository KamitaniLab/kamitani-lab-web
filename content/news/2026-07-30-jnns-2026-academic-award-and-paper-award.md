title: "JNNS 2026 Academic Award and Paper Award"
date: 2026-07-30
news_type: award

The Japanese Neural Network Society (JNNS) presented its 2026 awards to Kamitani Lab at the award ceremony held during NEURO2026 (Kobe International Conference Center, July 30, 2026).

**JNNS Academic Award (学術賞)** — Yukiyasu Kamitani

**JNNS Paper Award (論文賞)** — Shirakawa, K., Nagano, Y., Tanaka, M., Aoki, S. C., Muraki, Y., Majima, K., & Kamitani, Y. (2025). [Spurious reconstruction from brain activity](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2025.107515). *Neural Networks*, 190, 107515.
