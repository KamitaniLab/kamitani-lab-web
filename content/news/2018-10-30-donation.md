We accept donations to Kamitani Lab via Graduate School of Informatics Fund. Your support for our research is very much appreciated. 

On the Kyoto University Fund website:

[](https://payment.kikin.kyoto-u.ac.jp/kyoto-u_eng/entry.php)[https://payment.kikin.kyoto-u.ac.jp/kyoto-u_eng/entry.php](https://payment.kikin.kyoto-u.ac.jp/kyoto-u_eng/entry.php)

select “Graduate School of Informatics Fund” as “Purpose of Donation”.

After the web transaction is completed, please also email to the graduate office (infofund@i.kyoto-u.ac.jp) indicating that the donation is intended for supporting the research at Kamitani Lab (sorry for the inconvenience that you cannot state it on the web system).
