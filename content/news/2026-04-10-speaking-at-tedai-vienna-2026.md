Yukiyasu Kamitani will speak at [TEDAI Vienna 2026](https://tedai-vienna.ted.com/), Europe's official TED Conference on Artificial Intelligence, to be held at the Hofburg Palace in Vienna on October 28–30, 2026. He will appear alongside speakers such as Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw) and AI pioneer Geoffrey Hinton.
